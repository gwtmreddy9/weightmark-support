Weightmark

Privacy policy

Publisher: Gowtham Reddy Ambala · Contact: support@gwtmlabs.com · Effective November 11, 2026

What the app stores

Weightmark stores what you enter on your iPhone: your weight entries, their dates and notes, and your profile (name, height, date of birth, goal weight, units), your appearance settings and your reminder settings. These power your history, graph, streak, goal and BMI.

Weightmark has no account. It does not send any of this to the developer or to any server, and it has no advertising, tracking or analytics.

Apple Health

Apple Health is off until you connect it in Profile. Before iOS asks for permission, the app explains what connecting does. When connected, Weightmark:

  • Reads your body weight from Health, and nothing else. It adds the first reading of a day as that day's weigh-in, only for days that have none. iOS may open the app in the background to do this when Health receives a new weight.
  • Saves the weigh-ins you type in Weightmark to Health as your body weight, if you allow it. Editing or deleting such a weigh-in updates or removes the copy Weightmark saved. It cannot change data saved by other apps or devices.

Weights read from Health are stored on your iPhone with your other weigh-ins. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else, are not sold, and are not used for advertising or marketing. You can disconnect in Profile at any time; weigh-ins already added stay. You control the permissions themselves in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Weightmark.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Weightmark can remind you on a day you haven't logged, and tell you when weigh-ins are added from Apple Health. The reminder is off until you turn it on in Profile. All of them are created on your iPhone; no notification service or server is involved.

Export and import

Export makes a CSV file of your weigh-ins on your iPhone and opens the share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice, and Weightmark does not keep track of it. Import reads a CSV file you pick and adds its weigh-ins for days that have none.

Storage and backups

Your records live in the app's local storage on your iPhone. Weightmark has no cloud sync or backup service of its own. Your iPhone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) may include the app's data and restore it with your device; Apple and your device settings control those backups. If Apple Health is connected, the weigh-ins you type are also saved to Health, which keeps them under Apple's own Health data protections.

The app does not add its own encryption on top of your iPhone's. Use your iPhone's passcode and security features to protect it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, and edit or delete any weight entry in History. “Delete all weigh-ins” at the bottom of Profile removes every weigh-in from the iPhone after you confirm, and disconnects Apple Health; it does not change what is in Health. Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone; earlier device backups may still hold a copy. Because nothing is sent to the developer, lost records cannot be recovered by the developer.

If you email support

If you write to support@gwtmlabs.com, the developer, Cloudflare (which forwards the message) and Google (which provides Gmail) receive your email address and whatever you include. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask for yours to be deleted. Please don't send your weight history or other sensitive details unless they're needed.

Health information

Weightmark is a personal tracking tool. Its BMI information is general screening information; it does not diagnose any condition or provide treatment. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about health decisions.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated along with its effective date.
