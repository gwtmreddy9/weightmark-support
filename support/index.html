Today, Progress and Days

Today shows what you've eaten and today's weigh-in. Progress shows your weight trend, estimated daily burn and calorie target. Days lists your food and weight by day. Profile is the round button at the top right.

Logging or changing a weight

The first time you open the app each day, it offers a weigh-in after checking Apple Health. Type your weight on the keypad and tap Log, or tap Not now to dismiss it for that day. Today keeps a Log today's weight button until a weight is logged. Tap the weight chip to edit or delete it. Weightmark keeps one weigh-in per day.

Past days

Open Days and choose a month, then tap a day to open it in Today. Add food there, tap its weight chip to edit it, or use Add weight for this day. Add a past day opens the calendar.

Understanding Progress

Tap the chip at the top left to choose the last 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, year or all time. The graph needs two weigh-ins to draw a line; the trend smooths out day-to-day swings. Press and drag on the graph to read a day. Below it are your estimated burn, daily target, each week's calories next to its weight change, and BMI. BMI uses your height and latest weight; it is screening information, not a diagnosis.

Calorie target

In Profile, set your sex and activity level under Calories, and a goal weight and pace. With your height, birthday and a weigh-in, Progress shows an estimated daily burn and a daily target, and Today shows what you've eaten against the target.

The estimate starts from the US National Academies 2023 equations. After two weeks of logging food and weight, it uses your own numbers. The target takes off enough for your pace and never goes below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 for men. Tap Estimated burn in Progress for the details.

It is an estimate, not medical advice, and doesn't apply during pregnancy or breastfeeding. If you have or have had an eating disorder, talk to a doctor before following a calorie target.

Logging food

In Today, tap Search foods or the scan button beside it. Pick a meal and amount in servings, grams or ounces. Name search works offline; scanning needs camera permission and an internet connection. You can type a barcode into search and tap Look up barcode.

A recent chip adds that food's last amount to the current meal, with Undo for four seconds. Hold a recent chip to change the amount first. Tap a logged food to edit it, or hold to confirm deletion. Nutrition values can be incomplete or wrong; check the package when it matters.

Apple Health and smart scales

If your smart scale saves its readings to Apple Health, for example through the VeSync app, Weightmark can log your weight for you: step on the scale and the day's weigh-in appears. In the scale's own app, turn on sharing weight with Apple Health first.

In Profile, tap Apple Health, then Connect, and allow Weight when iOS asks. Weightmark adds the first body weight of each day from Health, for days with no weigh-in, and saves the weigh-ins you type to Health. New weights arrive on their own while your iPhone is unlocked; opening the app also checks.

If nothing arrives, open Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Weightmark and check that Weight is allowed. iOS does not tell the app when reading is refused, so a refusal looks the same as Health being empty.

Reminders and notifications

In Profile under Reminders, turn on the daily reminder and pick a time. The daily reminder only arrives on a day you haven't logged. If no notifications arrive, allow them in Settings > Notifications > Weightmark.

Export and import

Profile > Export weigh-ins makes a CSV file you can save or send. Import weigh-ins reads a CSV file with a date column (written like 2026-11-09) and a weight column named kg or lb. It only adds days that have no weigh-in.

Units, mode and theme

Tap the round profile button at the top right. Weight and height units are separate settings. Mode is Light, Dark or System; Theme is Minimal, or Vibrant in mint, lilac, sky or orange. Changes save as you make them.

Missing data

Your weigh-ins are stored on your iPhone; there is no account. Avoid deleting and reinstalling the app while troubleshooting missing history, and restore from your iPhone backup or an exported CSV file if needed.

Deleting everything

“Delete all weigh-ins” at the bottom of Profile removes every weigh-in after you type CONFIRM. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy. Food entries are not removed; delete them on Today.

Reporting a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Weightmark version, and the steps that cause the problem. Remove personal details from screenshots. Never send passwords or verification codes.
