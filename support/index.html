Logging or changing a weight

The first time you open the app each day, it offers a weigh-in after checking Apple Health. Type your weight on the keypad and tap Log, or tap Not now to close it for that day. Log keeps a Log today's weight button until a weight is in; once logged, tap the Weight row to change it. To see or fill in another day this week, tap that day in the row of circles. Weightmark keeps one weigh-in per day.

Editing or deleting a weigh-in

Open History and tap the entry to edit or delete it. A day you missed this week shows “No weigh-in” with an Add button, and “Add weigh-in” at the top adds any past day.

Understanding Progress

Choose Week, Month, 3 months, Year or All. The graph needs at least two weigh-ins in that period. The trend line smooths out day-to-day swings; the Trend switch turns it off. Press and drag on the graph to read a day. Your goal and BMI appear below. BMI uses your height and latest weight; it is general screening information, not a diagnosis.

Logging food

Open Log and tap +, or Add under a meal. Pick the meal, then search by name or tap the barcode button to scan a package. You can also type the number under a barcode into search and tap Look up barcode. Scanning needs camera access and an internet connection; name search works offline.

Choose an amount on the keypad, in servings, grams or ounces; switching units keeps the same amount. Tap a logged food to change its amount or meal, or press and hold it to delete it. Nutrition values come from public databases and product labels, so check the package when it matters.

Calorie target

Set your sex and activity during setup, or in Profile under Calories, where you can also choose a pace toward your goal. With your height, birthday and a weigh-in, Log shows what you've eaten against a daily target, such as 1,240/1,950 kcal. Without a goal weight, the target is your estimated burn.

The estimate starts from the US National Academies 2023 equations. After two weeks of logging food and weight, it uses your own numbers. The target never goes below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 for men. Tap Daily target in Profile for the details.

It is an estimate, not medical advice, and doesn't apply during pregnancy or breastfeeding. If you have or have had an eating disorder, talk to a doctor before following a calorie target.

Apple Health and smart scales

If your smart scale saves its readings to Apple Health, for example through the VeSync app, Weightmark can log your weight for you: step on the scale and the day's weigh-in appears. In the scale's own app, turn on sharing weight with Apple Health first.

In Profile, tap Apple Health, then Connect, and allow Weight when iOS asks. Weightmark adds the first body weight of each day from Health, for days with no weigh-in, and saves the weigh-ins you type to Health. New weights arrive on their own while your iPhone is unlocked; opening the app also checks.

If nothing arrives, open Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Weightmark and check that Weight is allowed. iOS does not tell the app when reading is refused, so a refusal looks the same as Health being empty.

Reminders and notifications

In Profile under Reminders, turn on the daily reminder and pick a time. The daily reminder only arrives on a day you haven't logged. If no notifications arrive, allow them in Settings > Notifications > Weightmark.

Export and import

Profile > Export weigh-ins makes a CSV file you can save or send. Import weigh-ins reads a CSV file with a date column (written like 2026-11-09) and a weight column named kg or lb. It only adds days that have no weigh-in.

Units, mode and theme

Tap the round profile button at the top right. Weight and height units are separate settings. Mode is Light, Dark or System; Theme is Minimal, or Vibrant in mint, lilac, sky or orange. Changes save as you make them.

Missing data

Your weigh-ins are stored on your iPhone; there is no account. Avoid deleting and reinstalling the app while troubleshooting missing history, and restore from your iPhone backup or an exported CSV file if needed.

Deleting everything

“Delete all weigh-ins” at the bottom of Profile removes every weigh-in after you type CONFIRM. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy. Food entries are not removed; delete them on the Log tab.

Reporting a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Weightmark version, and the steps that cause the problem. Remove personal details from screenshots. Never send passwords or verification codes.
